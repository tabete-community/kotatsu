As part of giving back to the software community, we offer a limited number of month-to-month support subscriptions to startups and small companies.

At a heavily rebated rate - **20% of our regular consulting rate** - you get 16 hours of senior technical support each month, without handing the work over to us.

## What you get

- **A monthly seat at the table**: at least one scheduled live session each month. The table is usually virtual, but we can occasionally meet in person when geography and circumstances make that practical.
- **Async and ad-hoc support**: use a dedicated Discord channel for questions and discussion, with short online meetings when a conversation is better than a thread.
- **Senior guidance**: help with DevOps, CI/CD, cloud infrastructure, automation, architecture and developer experience.
- **Flexible credits**: your subscription includes 16 support hours each month. Unused hours carry forward for up to 6 months, up to a maximum balance of 96 hours. Credits have no cash value and expire when the subscription ends.
- **A timely response**: we respond to new questions within one business day, normally during weekdays. This is an initial response or acknowledgement, not a promise to resolve the issue within that time.

## How we work

This is collaborative support, not outsourced engineering.

You and your team do the work and own the result. We provide guidance, experience, pairing, mentoring and a second pair of eyes.

We can help you get unstuck, make better technical decisions, understand unfamiliar infrastructure and build the confidence to maintain the result yourselves.

## What this is not

- Not a full-time engineer.
- Not an outsourced development team.
- Not production ownership.
- Not on-call or emergency support.
- Not a guarantee that we implement everything for you.

Sessions are arranged together and depend on reasonable availability. Support is limited to a small number of participating companies.

---

## No long-term commitment

This is intended to be easy to start and easy to stop.

The subscription runs month-to-month. You can cancel at any time; cancellation stops the next renewal, and access continues until the end of the current billing month.

Unused credits expire when the subscription ends and have no cash value.

We may occasionally need to pause or end a subscription if our available capacity changes.
